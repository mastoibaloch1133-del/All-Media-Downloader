Saving a Facebook video

  1. Find the video and tap Share.
  2. Tap Copy link, or copy the address from your browser’s address bar.
  3. Paste it in the field at the top and press Download.
  4. Select a quality and save the file.

Before you start

Facebook shows videos in Watch, on Pages, in Reels and in ordinary posts. If a video is public, the tool can look up its files, sometimes in more than one quality.

Anything limited to friends, a private group or a restricted audience is out of reach. The link from the Share menu gives the best chance of success.

Videos belong to their creators and Pages. Use the tool for your own uploads or for content you have been allowed to keep.

Facebook questions answered

Can private videos be saved?
No. Only public videos work. Group-only or friends-only videos cannot be accessed.
What qualities are offered?
Whatever Facebook makes available for that video, for example standard or HD.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes, in any mobile browser. Paste the link here and download.
Why does my link fail?
The video may not be public or may have been deleted, or the link is incomplete. Copy it again from Share.
Are videos stored on your site?
No. Nothing is hosted here. The file is fetched when you ask and saved to your device. See the Privacy Policy.
