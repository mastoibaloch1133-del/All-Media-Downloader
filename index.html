Questions

Common questions

What is Nazik Downloader?
It is a free web tool. You give it the link of a public video and it shows the files you can save, such as different qualities or an audio-only version. It does not host any videos itself.
Which sites does it work with?
TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook each have their own page here. Links from some other popular video sites may also work. If the tool cannot read a link, it tells you.
Do I need an account or an app?
No. Open the page in your browser, paste a link and download. There is nothing to install and nothing to sign up for.
Why can’t it read my link?
The video is probably private, deleted, age-restricted or limited to certain regions, or the link was cut off. Copy it again from the Share menu and try once more.
What happens to the links I paste?
They are sent to a third-party processing service that looks up the media files. Your recent-downloads list stays in your own browser and you can clear it at any time. The Privacy Policy has the details.
Can I download any video I like?
Only content you own or have permission to save. Platform rules and copyright still apply to you. See the Terms page for more.
