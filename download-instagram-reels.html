Saving a Reel or video post

  1. Open the Reel or video in Instagram.
  2. Tap the paper-plane icon or the ⋯ menu and choose Copy link.
  3. Paste the link into the field at the top and press Download.
  4. Choose a file from the list and save it.

Good to know

Reels use /reel/ links, regular posts use /p/ and older IGTV videos use /tv/. All three are accepted when the content is public.

If the account is private or the post has been taken down, there is nothing to fetch and you will see an error. When a link fails, confirm that the account is public and the post still exists.

The tool is meant for your own Reels, for backups, and for content you have the creator’s okay to keep. Please credit people whose work you share.

Questions

Instagram questions answered

Do Reels work?
Yes, public Reels can be read from their link.
Is an Instagram login needed?
No. You never type your Instagram username or password here.
Why does my link give an error?
The account may be private, the post removed or the link incomplete. Copy it once more.
Where do saved files go?
Usually to the Downloads folder or the Files app, depending on your browser and phone.
May I save anyone’s Reel?
Only with their permission. Respect creators and follow Instagram’s terms and copyright law.
