About Nazik Downloader

A small, free tool built by one developer.

Nazik Downloader was made by Ghullam Nazik Mastoi. The idea is simple: you paste the link of a public video, and the page shows the files that can be saved so you can keep a copy on your own device.

What you can do here

  • Use it from any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer, with nothing to install.
  • Save public videos from TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook, each with its own guide page.
  • See the available qualities, and audio-only files when the platform offers them, before you save.
  • Revisit your last few downloads from a list that lives only in your browser.

Limits

Only public content can be saved. Private, deleted, age-restricted or protected videos cannot be fetched, and what is offered for each video depends on the platform.

Using it fairly

Please save only what you own or have permission to keep, and credit creators. The Terms & DMCA page explains your responsibilities and how to report a problem.
