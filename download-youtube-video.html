Saving a YouTube video, step by step

  1. On YouTube, press Share under the video and choose Copy, or copy the address from your browser.
  2. Paste the link into the field at the top and press Download.
  3. Give it a moment while the list of files loads.
  4. Pick a resolution or an audio-only file and save it.

Things to know

Each YouTube video has its own set of files. The list you see after pasting a link is the real list for that video, so resolutions differ from video to video, and the highest ones are not always present.

Regular links, youtu.be short links and Share-menu links are accepted. Live streams, members-only content, age-restricted videos, and private or removed videos cannot be fetched.

YouTube’s terms usually forbid downloading unless the service provides a download option. Limit use to your own uploads, content under a licence that permits it, or videos you have permission to keep.

Questions

YouTube questions answered

How do I pick a quality?
Choose from the list shown after the link is read. Bigger resolutions make bigger files that take longer to save.
Is there an audio-only choice?
When one is available it appears among the formats. Select it instead of a video resolution.
Do I need extra software?
No. A normal browser is enough on a phone, tablet or computer.
Why was my video rejected?
Private, members-only, age-restricted, live and deleted videos are not supported, and region locks can block loading.
Can I download any YouTube video?
Only content you own or are allowed to save. Check YouTube’s terms and the creator’s rights. See our Terms page.
