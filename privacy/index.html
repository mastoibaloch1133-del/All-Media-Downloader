Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This page describes what happens to information when you use Nazik Downloader at nazik-downloader.pages.dev.

What we do not ask for

There are no accounts. You are never asked for a name, email address, phone number or password to use the downloader.

Links you paste

When you press Download, the link is passed to a third-party processing service that looks up the available media files. The chosen file is then fetched through that service and saved to your device. Avoid pasting links that contain private or sensitive details.

What stays in your browser

Your light or dark theme choice and up to five recent downloads (title, thumbnail link and file link) are kept in your browser’s local storage. They never leave your device through this site. Use “Clear all” or clear your browser data to remove them.

Services involved

  • Cloudflare Pages hosts the website and may keep ordinary server logs such as IP address and browser type.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver fonts and icons, so your browser contacts them when a page loads.
  • The media processing service described above reads the links you submit.
  • Google Search Console is used by the site owner to see how the site appears in search. It sets nothing on your device.

Cookies and advertising

This site sets no cookies of its own and runs no advertising or analytics scripts.

Children

The service is not aimed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Updates

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
